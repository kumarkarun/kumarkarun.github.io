About

I am part of Comcast's Applied AI Research group, where I lead Speech Machine Learning research. My work focuses on speech processing, multi-modal learning, and interpretability in deep learning — including research recognized with the Best Paper Award at ACL 2023.

I completed my MS in Computer Science at Syracuse University.
